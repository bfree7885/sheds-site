Sheds

Ethical, GIS-based shed hunting decision support.

Sheds is designed to support responsible shed hunting through geospatial analysis and seasonal awareness. It prioritizes wildlife welfare, land stewardship, and ethical field behavior over shortcuts or guarantees.

How Sheds Is Different

  • Focuses on when not to go as much as where to go
  • Enforces seasonal and ethical constraints by design
  • Provides decision support, not guarantees or shortcuts
