Sheds

GIS-based shed hunting decision support.

Sheds helps people make informed, responsible decisions about where and when to explore. It emphasizes seasonal awareness, land context, and wildlife sensitivity—prioritizing restraint and understanding.

What Sheds Will Provide

Sheds is designed to reduce guesswork around where and when it is appropriate to spend time in the field. Rather than focusing on finds or outcomes, it focuses on context—seasonal timing, land status, and environmental conditions that affect whether exploration makes sense at all.

Over time, Sheds will combine multiple spatial signals into a single, interpretable view so users can make calmer, more informed decisions before heading out. The goal is not to direct movement, but to support restraint, awareness, and intentional planning.

Access to these combined decision-support layers will eventually be available through a subscription model. This allows Sheds to remain independent, avoid advertising or data resale, and prioritize ethical constraints by design.

How Sheds Is Different

  • Focuses on when not to go as much as where to go
  • Enforces seasonal and ethical constraints by design
  • Provides decision support, not guarantees or shortcuts
Private development. Subscriber release planned.
