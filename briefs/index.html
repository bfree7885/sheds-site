Winter Weather Pressure and Shed Timing – 2026

Research Summary

Recent winter weather patterns across several regions have produced periods of deep snow followed by rapid thaw cycles. These conditions can influence deer movement and feeding patterns during late winter.

Key Findings

  • Deep snow reduces deer movement
  • Energy conservation increases bedding time
  • Feeding routes become more concentrated

Interpretation

When deer limit movement and concentrate in smaller terrain zones, shed distribution may become more clustered.

Sheds Take

Focus searches near winter bedding cover and south-facing slopes close to food sources.
