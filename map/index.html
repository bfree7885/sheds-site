      Field ethics & honesty
      
      
        
        
      • Privacy. GPS, Search Areas, observations, and sessions stay on-device; coordinates are not placed in share URLs or analytics. Weather/elevation queries send approximate lat/lng to Open-Meteo.
        • 
        
      • Property & law. This map does not grant legal access. Confirm permission and local shed seasons.
        • 
        
      • Wildlife. Do not chase, harass, or displace wintering deer. Approach bedding notes carefully.
        • 
        
      • Habitat. Avoid trampling sensitive areas; leave no trash; respect closures.
        • 
        
      • Privacy. Observations, tracks, and validations stay in this browser’s storage. Export creates a file you control.
        • 
        
      • Map providers. Basemap tile requests (Esri World Street / Topo / Imagery by default; never OSMF public raster) and optional Open-Meteo elevation/weather reveal approximate location to those services.
        • 
        
      • Model honesty. Timing, habitat, searchability, and evidence support are separate. Habitat uses real land cover/edge/slope inside your SEARCH AREA (or stays empty). Never a find probability.
        • 
        
      • Sensitive places. Do not publish precise private or protected locations from this tool.
