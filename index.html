Sheds

Terrain intelligence for shed hunting.

Sheds is a mapping and research platform that helps people understand where deer drop their antlers and why. Deer shed their antlers every winter as part of their natural cycle. This project combines wildlife biology, terrain analysis, and field observations to improve shed probability modeling.

Mission

We help shed hunters make better decisions by connecting terrain science with real-world deer behavior. Our goal is to build tools that teach ecology, support responsible hunting, and create a research-grade dataset for wildlife scientists.

Project Goals

  • Teach the ecological science behind shed hunting
  • Build a terrain-based navigation platform
  • Collect structured environmental field data
  • Improve terrain-based shed probability heatmaps
  • Create a research-grade dataset on shed distribution
  • Publish educational material and a future book based on findings
