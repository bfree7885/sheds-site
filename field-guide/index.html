Sheds Field Guide

Foundational principles for understanding whitetail behavior, terrain, and seasonal decision context. The Field Guide is evergreen. It does not chase headlines — it builds pattern recognition.

  • Thermal Cover Explained — Wind protection, conifer density, and microclimate.

  • Reading Edge Habitat — Transition zones and corridor logic.
  • When Not to Shed Hunt — Stress periods and ethical restraint.

The Field Guide complements Field Notes. Field Notes track current signals. The Field Guide explains the patterns behind them.
