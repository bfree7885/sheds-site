Winter Weather Pressure and Shed Timing – 2026

Research Summary

Winter monitoring reports indicate fluctuating snow depth and extended cold periods across several regions. These conditions influence deer movement and energy conservation.

Key Findings

  • Deep snow reduces daily movement
  • Deer increase bedding time in thermal cover
  • Movement corridors become concentrated

Interpretation

Concentrated deer movement may cluster shed distribution within specific terrain zones during late winter.

Sheds Take

Focus searches near south-facing slopes close to bedding areas and winter food sources.

Source: State Wildlife Monitoring Reports
