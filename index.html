Sheds

Terrain intelligence for shed hunting.

Sheds is a mapping and research platform designed to help people understand where deer drop their antlers and why. The project combines terrain analysis, wildlife biology, and field observations to build a better understanding of shed distribution across landscapes.

Field Guide → Field Notes → Field View

Project Goals

  • Teach the ecology and biology behind shed hunting
  • Build a terrain-based navigation platform
  • Collect structured environmental observations
  • Improve shed probability heatmaps using real field data
  • Create a research-quality dataset on shed distribution
  • Publish educational material and eventually a book based on the dataset

Heatmap Preview Layers

South Facing Slopes
Solar exposure and winter bedding patterns often concentrate deer activity on south-facing slopes during late winter.

Food Edges
Late winter feeding areas often produce sheds as deer move between food sources and bedding cover.

Travel Corridors
Terrain funnels, ridges, and creek crossings can concentrate deer movement.

Platform Sections

  • Field Guide — educational resources explaining the biology and terrain patterns behind shed hunting.
  • Field Notes — research summaries and current intelligence related to deer behavior, winter conditions, and shed timing.
  • Field View — an interactive map for navigation, terrain analysis, and shed logging.
